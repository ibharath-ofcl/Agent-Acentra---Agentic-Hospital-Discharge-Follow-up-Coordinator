Follow-up That Doesn’t Stop at Summarization

Most healthcare IT tools simply generate a static PDF summary. CareFlow AI establishes an active, closed-loop coordination lifecycle that tracks instructions through to verified resolution.

Static Paper Handout: Patient receives a multi-page summary with dense medical jargon.
Zero Outpatient Visibility: Hospital care team has no way of knowing if follow-ups were booked.
Silent Ambiguity: Missing specialist dates are overlooked until a post-discharge complication occurs.
High 30-Day Readmissions: Patients miss crucial cardiology, nephrology, or blood sugar checkpoints.
Result: Up to 20% preventable readmissions within 30 days.

Multi-Channel Reminder Engine

Gentle, Informational Follow-up Reminders

CareFlow AI schedules automated informational reminders to ensure patients never lose track of appointments or critical post-discharge lab milestones.

Strict Clinical Safety Boundary

AI reminders are strictly informational. The system must never diagnose, provide treatment advice, change medications, or answer medical questions. Any clinical inquiry by the patient immediately diverts to the human care team.

Clinical Safety & Governance Standard

Human in the Loop — Always

CareFlow AI organizes existing hospital instructions. It does not make autonomous clinical diagnoses or treatment decisions. When uncertainty is detected, the system does not guess — it routes directly to human clinical staff.

Clinical Boundary Matrix: System Capabilities vs Medical Authority

What CareFlow AI Automates (Administrative)
  • ✓EHR Record Ingestion: Reads and normalizes structured text from discharge summaries.
  • ✓Care Timeline Synthesis: Constructs chronological 30-day follow-up schedules.
  • ✓Informational Reminders: Sends scheduled phone call and SMS appointment notifications.
  • ✓Plain-Language Translation: Translates clinical instructions into patient-friendly languages.
  • ✓Provider Roster Matching: Identifies credentialed attending specialists in network.
What Requires Human Clinician Authority (Medical)
  • ✕Zero Medical Diagnosis: AI never diagnoses symptoms, interprets labs, or gives medical advice.
  • ✕No Prescription Changes: Medication dosage, additions, or discontinuations are strictly clinician-controlled.
  • ✕No Autonomous Date Guessing: Missing follow-up dates are never filled with hallucinated dates.
  • ✕Conflict Resolution: Contradictory surgical and physical therapy notes must be resolved by doctors.
  • ✕Emergency Escalation: Patient-reported red flag symptoms immediately trigger clinical alert.

5 Automated Escalation Triggers:

Missing information

e.g. Follow-up date or specialty clinic not specified in discharge summary

Ambiguous instructions

e.g. "Continue medication as discussed with team" without dosage

Conflicting instructions

e.g. Discharge note contradicts physical therapy weight-bearing order

Medication questions

e.g. Patient inquiries on dosing, side effects, or drug interactions

New or altered symptoms

e.g. Patient reports dizziness, chest tightness, or vitals variance post-discharge

Zero guesswork guarantee: Detected ambiguities automatically stop autonomous processing.
